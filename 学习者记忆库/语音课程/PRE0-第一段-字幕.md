# PRE0 第一段字幕

## English

Hello, and welcome to your first computer science lesson. I am your teacher, and we will build every idea from the very beginning. We will go slowly, practise often, and celebrate each small discovery.

## 中文翻译

你好，欢迎来到你的第一节计算机课。我会像老师一样陪你从最基础开始建立每一个概念。我们会慢慢来，经常练习，也会认真记住每一个小发现。

## English

Today’s question is simple: what does a computer do when you press a key? The key idea is input, process, and output.

## 中文翻译

今天的问题很简单：当你按下一个按键时，电脑到底做了什么？核心思想是：输入、处理和输出。

## English

Input means information entering the computer. Process means the computer works on that information. Output means the result comes back to you, perhaps on a screen or through a speaker.

## 中文翻译

Input，也就是输入，指信息进入电脑。Process，也就是处理，指电脑对这些信息进行工作。Output，也就是输出，指结果回到你这里，可能显示在屏幕上，也可能从音箱播放出来。

## Pause and predict

When you type the letter A, what is the input, what is the process, and what is the output? Say your answer aloud before continuing.

当你输入字母 A 时，什么是输入，什么是处理，什么是输出？继续之前，请先用自己的话说出答案。

## Five words

`input` 输入 · `process` 处理 · `memory` 内存 · `storage` 存储 · `output` 输出

## Memory sentence

Every computer action has an input, a change of state, and an observable output.

每一个电脑动作，都有输入、状态变化，以及可以观察到的输出。
